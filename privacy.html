Privacy

Keywren runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, and nothing you type, say or show to your camera is ever uploaded to us or stored on any server.

Camera and microphone

The mic and webcam tests only ask for access after you press Start, and use it only inside the open tab. Audio levels, waveforms, recordings, video and snapshots are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere. Recordings and snapshots live in memory and are deleted when you leave the page; a file is only saved if you click download. The camera and microphone are switched off when you press Stop, switch tabs or leave the page. You can revoke access at any time from your browser's site settings.

Keyboard and mouse

The typing test, keyboard tester and mouse test read key presses and clicks only inside this page to show your results. Keystrokes are never recorded beyond the current test and never leave your device.

What's stored on your device

Your browser's local storage keeps: your last 50 typing results and typing options, your best click-speed (CPS) scores and test length, your webcam resolution and mirror choice, and your cookie choice. You can clear typing history on the typing page, or delete everything by clearing site data for this site in your browser.

Advertising

Keywren is free because it shows ads. Ad partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. If you choose "Essential only", only non-personalised ads are shown. Ads never have access to your camera, microphone, keystrokes or results. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

Hosting logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability.

Children

Keywren is a general audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

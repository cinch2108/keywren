How to use the Mouse test

  1. Choose a CPS test length: 1, 5, 10 or 30 seconds.
  2. Click inside the big box as fast as you can. The timer starts on your first click and your clicks per second appear when it ends.
  3. Your best score for each length is saved in your browser. Click again to retry.
  4. In the tester below, press each button inside the box, including middle, back and forward. Scroll up and down to test the wheel.
  5. Single-click slowly a few times. If “Suspected chatter” goes up, your mouse may be registering double clicks on its own.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CPS score?

Most people click 6–7 times per second. 8–10 CPS is fast, and advanced techniques like jitter clicking or butterfly clicking can reach 12–20 CPS. Short tests give higher numbers than 30-second tests because your hand tires.

How do I know if my mouse double clicks by itself?

Click once, slowly, many times in the tester. A healthy switch registers one press per click. If Keywren sees two presses of the same button less than 80 ms apart, it counts it as suspected chatter, which usually means the switch is wearing out.

Why don’t my side buttons work?

Back and forward buttons are buttons 4 and 5. Some mice need their own software to send them, and some are remapped to keyboard shortcuts. If the tester shows nothing, check your mouse software or try another USB port.

Will clicking here navigate away or open menus?

No. Inside the test boxes, right-click menus, middle-click scrolling and back/forward navigation are blocked so you can test every button safely.

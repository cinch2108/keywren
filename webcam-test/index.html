How to use the Webcam test

  1. Press Start webcam test and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. Choose a resolution to request (720p by default). The readout shows what the camera actually delivers.
  3. Watch Measured FPS for a few seconds. Low light often makes webcams drop to 15 FPS or less.
  4. Use the device list to switch between cameras, and untick Mirror to see the image the way others see it.
  5. Press Take snapshot to save a full-resolution PNG. Press Stop when done; the camera also turns off when you switch tabs.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my webcam not working?

Check that no other app (Zoom, Teams, the Camera app) is using it, that your browser has camera permission for this site, and on laptops that there is no privacy shutter or camera key turned off. Then press Start again or pick another camera from the list.

Why is the resolution lower than my camera’s spec?

The browser picks the closest mode the camera offers. Many webcams only reach their top resolution at a lower frame rate, or deliver 1080p only over USB 3. Try a different resolution, a different USB port, or better lighting.

Why is my frame rate low?

In dim light, webcams lengthen the exposure, which lowers the frame rate. Busy USB hubs and power saving can also reduce it. Add light in front of you and check Measured FPS again.

Are snapshots or video uploaded?

No. The video is shown directly from your camera inside this page, and snapshots are created in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere; you choose whether to download a snapshot.

How to use the Mic test

  1. Press Start mic test and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  2. Speak normally. The meter and waveform should move; aim for “Good level”, around −30 to −12 dB on speech.
  3. If nothing moves, choose a different microphone in the device list. Headsets and webcams each show up as their own microphone.
  4. Pick a length and press Record, then play the clip back to hear echo, noise or distortion.
  5. Press Stop when you are done. The mic also turns off by itself when you switch tabs or leave the page.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my browser say the microphone is blocked?

You (or a setting) denied permission earlier. Click the lock or camera icon in the address bar, set Microphone to Allow for this site and press Start mic test again. On macOS, also check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. The level meter and waveform are computed in your browser with the Web Audio API, and recordings are stored in memory in this tab only. Nothing is sent to a server, and the recording is deleted when you leave the page.

What is a good microphone level?

For speech, peaks around −12 dB and an average around −20 to −30 dB are ideal. If the meter shows “Clipping” your voice will distort, so move back or lower the input gain. “Quiet” means people may struggle to hear you.

Why is echo cancellation turned off?

Keywren asks for the raw microphone signal so you hear what the mic really picks up. Video call apps usually turn on echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain, so you may sound slightly different in a call.

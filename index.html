How to use the Typing Speed test

  1. Pick a test length: 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds. Turn on punctuation or numbers for a harder test.
  2. Click the text box (or just start typing). The clock starts on your first keystroke.
  3. Type the words you see. Correct letters turn white, wrong ones turn red. Press Backspace to fix a mistake.
  4. When time runs out you see your WPM, raw WPM, accuracy and correct and wrong characters.
  5. Press Esc or Try again for a fresh text. Your results history and personal best are saved in this browser.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Keywren counts every correctly typed character, including spaces, divides by 5 (the standard length of a “word”) and then by the minutes taken. Raw WPM counts every character you typed, right or wrong, so the gap between the two shows how much mistakes cost you.

What is a good typing speed?

Most people type 35–45 WPM. 60 WPM is fast and comfortable for office work, 80 WPM and above is very fast, and competitive typists reach 120 WPM or more. Accuracy above 95% matters more than raw speed.

How is accuracy measured?

Accuracy is the share of your keystrokes that were correct when you made them. Fixing a mistake with Backspace still counts the original error, so accuracy shows how cleanly you type, not just the final text.

Are my results uploaded anywhere?

No. The test runs entirely in your browser and your last 50 results are kept only in this browser’s local storage. Clear history removes them.
